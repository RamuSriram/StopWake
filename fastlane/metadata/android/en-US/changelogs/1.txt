• Initial public release of StopWake v1.0.
• Smart wake-up mode (gentle vibration when screen is unlocked vs. full alarm when locked).
• Dark OpenStreetMap view with offline tile caching and custom wake-up radius (50m–5,000m).
• Automatic recurring day-of-week commute schedules.
• JSON stop and schedule sharing for friends on the same route.
• Optional emergency SMS safety net on overshoot.
